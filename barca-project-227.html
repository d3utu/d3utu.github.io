Maintenance Calculation (Oxford/Henry equation)

Method: julienvenesson.fr energy calculator, Oxford/Henry (2005) equation.

Step 1 — BMR (unisex formula: 14.2 × weight(kg) + 593)

  • Inputs: 23 y/o, male, 174.4 cm, 71.90 kg
  • BMR = 14.2 × 71.9 + 593 = ~1614 kcal

Step 2 — Activity level (baseline, excluding cardio)

  • Baseline now counts only: software engineering job (desk-based), 10k daily steps, and lifting (2 upper + 2 lower + 3 accessory = 7 sessions/week, low set volume). Cardio is handled separately below as a deficit lever.
  • Selected tier: "Modérément actif"

Step 3 — Cardio's contribution to the deficit (MET estimate: kcal = MET × weight(kg) × hours)

  • Low-intensity cardio: ~4.5 MET × 71.9kg × 0.5h = ~162 kcal/session × 3/week = 485 kcal/week
  • High-intensity cardio (football/padel): ~7.5 MET × 71.9kg × 1h = ~539 kcal/session × 2/week = 1078 kcal/week
  • Combined weekly cardio burn: ~1563 kcal/week → ~223 kcal/day averaged across all 7 days

Step 4 — Building the 550 kcal/day deficit from both levers

  • Cardio supplies: ~223 kcal/day
  • Diet needs to supply the rest: 550 − 223 = ~327 kcal/day
  • Food target = 2502 − 327 ≈ 2175 kcal/day
